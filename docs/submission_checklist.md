# Submission checklist: desymmetrization diagnostic

**Manuscript:** [`desymmetrisation_paper.md`](desymmetrisation_paper.md)
**Status:** draft complete and reproducible; **not submitted**, no venue assigned, no
review. The checkboxes below are the author's actions, not claims that they are done.

## 1. Venue

| venue | type | fit | why |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| arXiv (quant-ph) | preprint | first step | establishes priority; invites feedback |
| Physical Review E | journal | high | standard venue for quantum chaos; the reference [1] lives there |
| Chaos (AIP) | journal | high | methodological diagnostics are in scope |
| Journal of Physics A | journal | medium | mathematical physics; the posterior model fits |
| SciPost Physics | journal | medium | open review; good for methods notes |

Recommended sequence: arXiv first, then Physical Review E; if rejected, revise for Chaos.

Do not submit to a mathematics journal — the manuscript is about a numerical diagnostic,
not a theorem — and do not claim a result about the Riemann hypothesis.

## 3. Figures

All four are generated by one script and committed to the repository:

```bash
python scripts/generate_desymmetrisation_figures.py
```

| figure | file | content | status |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Fig. 1 | `output/desymmetrisation.png` | `⟨r⟩` vs unresolved sectors, 300 seeds | generated |
| Fig. 2 | `output/desymmetrisation_warning.png` | per-spectrum scatter, four sector counts, both classes | generated |
| Fig. 3 | `output/desymmetrisation_coverage.png` | measured coverage vs nominal 95% | generated |
| Fig. 4 | `output/desymmetrisation_flow.png` | sector populations, commuting vs cross-sector start | generated |

Fig. 1 is produced by `scripts/run_desymmetrisation_demo.py`; Figs. 2–4 by
`scripts/generate_desymmetrisation_figures.py`. Every number plotted is computed at draw
time.

Journal requirements:

| venue | format | resolution | colour |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| arXiv | PNG or PDF | `≥ 150 dpi` | colour acceptable |
| Physical Review E | EPS or PDF | vector preferred | colour acceptable; must read in greyscale |
| Chaos | EPS or PDF | vector preferred | colour acceptable |

Note for the greyscale requirement: Figs. 2 and 4 distinguish classes by colour and
marker; the markers differ (circle/square) and the line styles differ (solid/dashed), so
the figures survive greyscale, but this should be checked visually before submission.

## 8. If it is rejected

| stated reason | response |
|:---|:---|
| "not novel enough" | emphasize the measured coverage and the posterior, not the phenomenon |
| "not relevant here" | submit to Chaos or J. Phys. A |
| "the limits are too severe" | the limits are *measured*; that is the contribution |
| "no comparison to explicit desymmetrization" | §6 already makes the comparison |
| "the Lean verification is not relevant" | remove it from the manuscript, keep it in the repository |

A rejection for "not being about the Riemann hypothesis" is a rejection by the wrong
venue, and is a reason to change venue rather than to change the paper.
